The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Day-first tenants (every tenant on file) get day and month swapped: a 7 April booking goes out on 4 July. This is the bug #4471 describes, and the fix that is queued.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells day-first people to type month first. Its date field shows MM/DD/YYYY, people type what it says, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way. The swap looks the same as parser, but the cause is the form’s hint, so the parser fix does not touch it. Only form reports from tenants on booking-v3 have it. Intake tags it from the reporter’s browser, which is often wrong; the table counts by tenant and channel (causes.ts).

one-day-move: the date moves by one day, not a swap. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” “Every booking in June is one day off.” Nothing is swapped, so neither fix covers it. The pattern points at time zones or the change to summer time. #4471’s table files these under parser by tenant and keeps them in a footnote, so no ticket ever tracked them.
